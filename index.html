Kaggle highlight

Predictive Modeling for Healthcare Costs in Vietnam — 12th of 78 teams

A complete competition run on Vietnamese social health insurance records: predicting a year of individual healthcare cost (RMSE). Five submissions improved the public leaderboard score from 2.69057 to 2.25342 (as of Nov 25, 2026). The gains came from feature engineering, not tuning: the target is exactly log1p of outpatient + inpatient cost and 81.7% of rows are zero, and households are split across train and test — so the winning features were leakage-safe household statistics (leave-one-out for training rows, recomputed inside each CV fold) and segment target encoding over ID-prefix blocks. A final round (v6) added household age-composition features and a CatBoost hurdle model.
